Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026 · Applies to the Bad Delivery Inc. game for iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play)
Bad Delivery Inc. does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send anything from your phone to us or to anyone else. Its optional in-app purchases are paid and processed entirely by the App Store or Google Play.

This policy explains what that means in practice. "We" and "us" means Ahmet Arif Öz, the developer of the game.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any device or advertising identifier, and it contains no code that sends data off your device. The Android version does not even request permission to use the internet. It asks for two permissions: vibration, for the optional haptic feedback, and Google Play billing, which lets it open Google Play's own purchase window.

In-app purchases

The game's SHOP sells optional coin packs and vehicle bundles. You pay through Apple's App Store or Google Play, under their terms and privacy policies, and only after you confirm the purchase in their payment window. We never receive your name, email address, payment card or store account details.

To deliver what you bought, the game receives from the store on your phone which item was bought and a transaction identifier. It keeps a record of delivered items and transactions in its storage on your phone, so that nothing is added twice, and does not send it anywhere. Apple and Google give developers sales reports and order records (for example the item, the price, the country and an order number) for accounting, refunds and support; they provide these under their own privacy policies.

Information stored on your phone

To remember your progress, the game saves a few values in your phone's standard app storage:

  • coins, stars, which jobs are unlocked, and the vehicles and upgrades you own;
  • the record of delivered purchases described above;
  • whether you have seen the introduction;
  • your settings (sound and vibration on or off).

This data stays on your phone. On iPhone it is included in your own iPhone or iCloud backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy. On Android it may be included in your device backup if you have turned backup on, which Google handles under its own privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Third-party services

The game is built with the Unity engine and uses Unity In-App Purchasing only to talk to the App Store or Google Play on your phone. It includes no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution or tracking SDKs, and Unity's optional online services (Unity Gaming Services, analytics, ads, crash reporting and diagnostics) are not used.

Information Apple or Google may share with us

If you choose to share crash reports and usage data with app developers (on iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements; on Android: the "usage & diagnostics" setting), Apple or Google may give us anonymous, aggregated crash and performance statistics. They provide these under their own privacy policies; the statistics do not identify you, and we use them only to fix problems.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either. Every purchase has to be confirmed in the store's payment window, and parents can require their approval for purchases with Apple's Ask to Buy or Google Play's purchase approval settings.

Your choices

As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can reset your game progress at any time by deleting the app. For a refund or your purchase history, contact the store you bought from.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If the game ever starts collecting data, we will say so here and in the App Store and Google Play listings before it does.
