Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026 · Applies to the Bad Delivery Inc. game for iPhone
Bad Delivery Inc. does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it does not send anything from your iPhone to us or to anyone else.

This policy explains what that means in practice. "We" and "us" means Ahmet Arif Öz, the developer of the game.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any device or advertising identifier, and it contains no code that sends data off your device.

Information stored on your iPhone

To remember your progress, the game saves a few values in your iPhone's standard app storage:

  • coins, stars, which jobs are unlocked, and the vehicles and upgrades you own;
  • whether you have seen the introduction;
  • your settings (sound and vibration on or off).

This data stays on your iPhone. It is included in your own iPhone or iCloud backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Third-party services

The game is built with the Unity engine. It includes no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution or tracking SDKs, and Unity's optional online services (analytics, ads, crash reporting) are switched off.

Information Apple may share with us

If you choose to share crash reports and app analytics with app developers (iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated crash and usage statistics. Apple provides these under its own privacy policy; they do not identify you, and we use them only to fix problems.

Children

The game is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 either.

Your choices

As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can reset your game progress at any time by deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If the game ever starts collecting data, we will say so here and in the App Store listing before it does.
