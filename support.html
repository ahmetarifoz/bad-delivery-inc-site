Help for the Bad Delivery Inc. game for iPhone

Contact us

Email ahmetarifoz.aaz@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

If something went wrong, it helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version and the game version (shown at the bottom of the game's SETTINGS card), plus the job you were playing.

How do I play?

The van drives itself. Steer with the arrow pads, hold BRAKE to slow down or back up, and hold DRIFT to swing round tight corners. Before each job, choose where the cargo rides and up to two pieces of gear to hold it still. The less damage the cargo takes, the more stars and coins you earn. To see the introduction again, open SETTINGS and tap HOW TO PLAY.

How do I unlock the next job?

Jobs open one at a time. Deliver the current job with at least one star and the next one unlocks.

What are coins for?

Coins from deliveries buy vehicles in the GARAGE and upgrades in UPGRADES. Cargo upgrades such as the anti-slip mat, straps and foam only help when that gear is fitted on the LOAD CARGO screen.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Open SETTINGS (on the title screen, or the cog at the top of the menus) and tap SOUND or VIBRATION. The game also stays quiet when your iPhone's silent switch is on.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your iPhone as you play and is included in your iPhone or iCloud backups. Deleting the app deletes your progress. There is no online account, so progress does not move between devices.

Are there purchases or ads?

No. The game has no in-app purchases, no ads and no account.
